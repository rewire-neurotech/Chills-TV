Privacy Policy

Rewire — by ReWire Neurotechnologies, Inc. · Effective date: 08/01/2026 · Version 1.0

This Privacy Policy explains how ReWire Neurotechnologies, Inc. (“ReWire,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you use the Rewire application and related services (the “Service”). It is part of our Terms & Conditions. If you do not agree with it, do not use the Service.

The Service is built around information you choose to share about your inner life. We designed our data practices around that reality: we collect what the Service needs to work, we do not sell your information, and we do not use the content of your reflections for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. Email address, password (stored as a hash), and subscription status.

Your reflections and inputs (“User Content”). The text you provide about what you want to meditate on and why it matters to you. This is the core of the Service and can include deeply personal information about your emotional state, relationships, memories, and wellbeing. You control what you share — the Service works with whatever level of detail you’re comfortable providing.

Experience responses. Your answers to in-app reflection questions, such as whether an experience gave you chills, and any journal entries you write.

Generated content. The experiences (script and audio) the Service generates for you.

Usage and device information. Session timestamps, features used, device type, browser, and IP address, used to secure and debug the Service. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Payment information. Payments are processed by Stripe. We receive confirmation of your purchase and subscription status; we do not receive or store your full card number.

2. Consumer Health Data and Sensitive Information

Some of the information described above — your reflections, your emotional responses, and your journal entries — may reveal information about your mental or emotional state. Depending on where you live, this may be treated as “sensitive personal information” (California) or “consumer health data” (Washington, Nevada, and other states).

Your consent. We collect and process this information only with your affirmative consent, which you give on the “Before you begin” screen when you create your account. We use it solely to provide the Service to you, as described in this Policy.

What we collect and why: the reflections and responses listed in Section 1, used to generate your personalized experiences, operate the daily protocol format, and maintain your journal.

Sources: you. We do not collect health-related information about you from third parties.

Sharing: shared only with the service providers listed in Section 4, only as needed to generate and deliver your experiences. We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it for advertising. We will never sell or share your consumer health data in exchange for anything of value without the separate, valid authorization required by applicable law.

Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time by deleting specific journal entries or protocols, or by deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account), which removes the associated data as described in Section 6. Because the Service is built around this information, withdrawing consent means some or all of the Service will stop working for you.

Washington and Nevada residents: you have the right to confirm whether we collect consumer health data about you, to access it, to withdraw consent, to have it deleted, and to appeal a refusal, as described in Section 7. A list of the third parties with whom consumer health data is shared appears in Section 4.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Generate your personalized experiences, including by processing your inputs through the AI and voice-synthesis providers listed in Section 4.
  • Operate your account, protocols, journal, and subscription.
  • Run safety screening on inputs before an experience is generated, and surface crisis resources where appropriate.
  • Maintain, secure, debug, and improve the Service.
  • Communicate with you about your account, purchases, and material changes to the Service or our terms.
  • Comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do not use the content of your reflections for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Who We Share Information With

We share information only with:

Service providers, bound by contracts limiting their use of your data to providing services to us:

  • AI text generation and safety screening: large language model providers (Anthropic, Google)
  • Voice synthesis: ElevenLabs
  • Hosting and infrastructure: Render
  • Payments: Stripe
  • Email delivery: our transactional email provider

Our AI and voice providers process your inputs to generate your experiences and do not use them to train their models, under the terms of our agreements with them.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law (for example, a valid subpoena), or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of you, ReWire, or others. Where legally permitted, we will attempt to notify you of legal demands for your data.

Business transfers. If ReWire is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction; this Policy will continue to apply to it, and we will notify you of any change in ownership or use.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including encryption in transit and access controls that limit who at ReWire can see User Content. No system is perfectly secure; please use a strong, unique password and keep your device secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information while your account is active. You can delete individual journal entries and protocols in the app at any time.

When you delete your account (Settings → Delete my account), we delete your account information, User Content, experience responses, and generated content within 10 days, except where we must retain limited records to comply with law (for example, payment and consent records) or to resolve disputes.

You can also export your data at any time (Settings → Download my data).

7. Your Rights

Everyone. Regardless of where you live, you can access, export, correct, and delete your information through the app (Settings), or by emailing hello@rewire.bio. We do not discriminate against you for exercising any privacy right.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to access and receive a portable copy; to correct inaccuracies; to delete; and to limit the use of sensitive personal information. We use sensitive personal information only to provide the Service you request, and we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CPRA, so no sale/share opt-out is required. To exercise rights, use the in-app tools or email hello@rewire.bio; we will verify requests using your account email. You may use an authorized agent as permitted by law.

Washington and Nevada residents (consumer health data laws). In addition to the rights above, you may confirm collection of, access, and delete consumer health data, and withdraw consent, as described in Section 2. If we decline a request, you may appeal by replying to our decision email with “Appeal” in the subject line; we will respond within the legally required period.

Where the Service is available. The Service is offered only to users located in the United States, and all data is processed in the United States. The Service is not directed to, and is not available in, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from residents of those regions. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

8. Children

The Service is for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn we have, we will delete it. If you believe a minor has used the Service, contact hello@rewire.bio.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes — especially any change to how we handle your reflections or consumer health data — we will notify you in the app and ask for your renewed consent where the law requires it. The effective date at the top shows when this Policy was last updated, and prior versions are available on request.
